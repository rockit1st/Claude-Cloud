Checking…

Use the printed Kitronik guide too. It is the booklet in the box (version V1.4). It has the exact steps and the pictures. This page is a checklist to help you. If this page and the booklet are different, the booklet is right.

1. Check your parts

Open the box. Tick each part you find.

You may need to bring or ask for these

These may not be in the box. Check, and ask Mr. Capellini.

The ticks are only for you. They are not saved when you close the page.

2. Build in order

Do one step at a time.

  1. Fold the chassisCut and fold the cardboard chassis into shape. Take your time and fold along the lines.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  2. Put on the motorsFix the two small motors to the chassis.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  3. Put on the wheelsPush a wheel with its tyre onto each motor.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  4. Add the ballAdd the ping pong ball. It helps the robot roll.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  5. Use the rubber bandUse the rubber band where the booklet shows.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  6. Connect the motorsUse the clip leads to join each motor to Motor 1 or Motor 2 on the Klip Motor Driver. Do not guess. Follow the booklet.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  7. Plug in the micro:bitSlide the micro:bit into the Klip Motor Driver, the right way round.Follow the booklet page for this step.
  8. Add powerAdd power the way the booklet says. Ask Mr. Capellini before you switch on.Follow the booklet page for this step.

Your booklet may put the steps in a slightly different order. If it does, follow the booklet.

3. Check it works

⚠️ Stay safe

  • Scissors: cut away from your body. Walk slowly with them.
  • Batteries: do not mix old and new. If a battery feels hot or smells strange, tell Mr. Capellini right away.
  • Clips: never touch the metal ends of the clip leads together.
  • Power: ask before you switch it on.
  • Hands: keep fingers and hair away from the wheels when the robot is on.
