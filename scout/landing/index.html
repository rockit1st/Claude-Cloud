How it works

Three steps. Then you're done checking.

Setting up a scout takes about thirty seconds. After that, the hunt runs itself.

Type what you're hunting — Amazing Spider-Man #1 CGC, Hot Toys Iron Man MK III, Jordan '86 Fleer rookie. Add a price range if you have a number in mind. That's the whole setup.

Features

Built for people who know exactly what they want

Scout isn't a browsing app. It's a precision instrument for the one item you're actually after.

Every marketplace, one search

eBay, Reddit's buy/sell subreddits, and Chrono24 — covered by a single scout. No more tab cycling, no more "did I check r/comicswap today?"

Instant push, per-scout control

Get notified the moment a match goes live. Loud alerts for the grail, silent mode for the maybes — every scout has its own notification toggle.

A dashboard of every find

Prefer to browse on your own schedule? Every match lands in your dashboard, newest first — review the morning's finds over coffee instead of getting buzzed all day.

Any item, any category

Graded comics, vintage toys, trading cards, watches, sneakers, synths, fountain pens. If someone might list it, you can scout it. Keywords are the only limit.

Price range, if you want it

Optionally cap your budget — or set a floor to filter out the parts-only junk. Scout only pings you on listings that fit your numbers.

On duty 24/7

Scouts run in the cloud, not on your phone. Battery untouched, coverage uninterrupted — your scout is watching while you sleep, work, or live your life.
