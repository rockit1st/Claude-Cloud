How It Works

altitude = distance × tan(angle) + eye height
Step 1
Measure Distance
Stand at a measured spot (your teacher's station or a measuring tape). Best spot: about as far away as you think the rocket will fly.
Step 2
Read the Inclinometer
Aim the straw at the rocket at its highest point, pinch the string and read the number. Type that string reading — the calculator does 90° − reading for you.
Step 3
Apply Trigonometry
You, the launch pad and the rocket make a right triangle. Tangent tells you how many feet your line of sight climbs for every foot you stood away.
Step 4
Add Your Eye Height
You measured from your eyes, not the ground, so add how high your eyes are (about 5 ft). The answer is how high the rocket flew above the launch pad.
